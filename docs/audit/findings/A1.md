## Khuyen nghi Batch Run: tab trong Lab hay trang nav?

Chon: **tab/che do ben trong Lab** ("Cham diem 1 kich ban" | "Chay hang loat tu Excel/CSV"), giu route `/batch-run` lam deep link (alias -> `/lab?mode=batch`).
- Dung mental model: Batch chay 1 workflow tren nhieu dong du lieu, cung picker workflow, cung "San pham"/ket qua voi Lab; luong vao duy nhat tu Lab roi.
- Them 1 muc nav se lam Sidebar day them (da co Home/Tutorial/Lab/Workflow/Settings) tren mobile.
- Chi phi: BatchRunView 2061 dong, co WebSocket rieng va layout `launch-view` toan man hinh; khong nen nhet nguyen khoi vao LabView (1.5k dong). Buoc 1 (S, lam ngay): bo `target=_blank`, cho Sidebar hien tren /batch-run, them link "Quay lai Lab". Buoc 2 (M): dua thanh tab Lab bang lazy component, route cu redirect.
- Neu khong lam tab: toi thieu phai co breadcrumb "Lab > Chay hang loat" + Sidebar, khong de trang cut.
